      How to play

      Goal

      Be the first player to get rid of all your cards. The moment someone plays their last card, they win the game.

      Setup

      2–8 players, 108 cards. Everyone gets 7 cards; the top card of the deck starts the discard pile. Play begins to the dealer’s left and goes clockwise.

      Your turn

      Match the top card of the discard pile by color, number or symbol, or play a Wild. If you can’t (or don’t want to) play, draw one card. If that card can be played you may play it right away — otherwise your turn ends.

      Action cards

      • Skip — the next player loses their turn.
      • Reverse — play changes direction. With 2 players it works like Skip.
      • Draw Two — the next player must stack another +2 or draw 2 and lose their turn.
      • Wild — play any time and choose the next color. The card takes on that color on the pile.
      • Wild Draw Four — play any time and choose the color; the next player must stack another +4 or draw 4 and lose their turn. It can’t be challenged.

      7-0 rule

      • 7 — swap hands with a player of your choice: click their seat. Your 10-second clock restarts for the choice; if it runs out, a random opponent is picked. With 2 players you swap straight away.
      • 0 — everyone passes their hand to the next player in the direction of play.

      If a swap leaves someone holding one card, they count as having called UNO.

      Stacking +2 and +4

      When a +2 is played on you, you can answer with a +2 of your own (any color); when a +4 is played on you, you can answer with a +4. The two don’t mix — a +4 can’t go on a +2 stack, or the other way round. The total keeps growing (+2, +4, +6… or +4, +8, +12…) and passes to the next player. If you can stack, you choose: add your card, or press Take (or click the deck) to draw the whole total and lose your turn — holding a matching card doesn’t force you to use it. If you have nothing to stack, you take the total automatically.

      Turn timer

      You have 10 seconds to move. If time runs out you draw one card (kept even if it could be played) and play passes on. If a stack was waiting for you, you take the whole stack instead.

      UNO!

      Press UNO! before you play down to one card — it only lights up on your turn when you hold two cards and can play one of them. Once you’ve played your second-to-last card without calling it, it’s too late: you can’t call UNO any more. If another player catches you with one card before the next player starts their turn — by pressing Catch! on your seat — you draw 2.

      First card

      Wild Draw Four is reshuffled. Wild: the first player picks the color. Skip: the first player is skipped. Reverse: the dealer plays first and play goes counter-clockwise. Draw Two: the first player draws 2 and is skipped.
